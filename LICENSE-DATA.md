# Data licence

The data in `data/`, the tables in `outputs/` and the figures in `figures/` are licensed under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

You may share and adapt them for any purpose, provided you credit the source by citing the paper (see `CITATION.cff`).

The accident information is compiled from the public investigation reports of the National Transportation Safety
Committee of Indonesia (Komite Nasional Keselamatan Transportasi, KNKT), https://knkt.go.id.

The base map in `data/basemap_naturalearth_10m.geojson` is derived from Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com),
which is in the public domain.

The SHIELD factor definitions in `data/shield_codebook_stroeve2023.csv` are quoted from Stroeve, S.; Kirwan, B.;
Turan, O.; et al. SHIELD Human Factors Taxonomy and Database for Learning from Aviation and Maritime Safety
Occurrences. *Safety* **2023**, *9*, 14. https://doi.org/10.3390/safety9010014. That article is published under
CC BY 4.0.
